## API shape

- hcli talks to the Robot webservice at https://robot-ws.your-server.de/ through WWW::Hetzner. Personalities are read-only: ip, servers, reset, subnet, rdns, sshkey, products, storagebox, server_transactions, market_transactions, traffic, market_search.
- hmarket does not call that API. It filters the public server-auction JSON. Default path keeps is_ecc and lines matching /Enterprise HDD/.
- Hetzner publishes no Robot or Cloud sandbox. Do not point regress at the live account.
